# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The owner and a small team of designers and machine operators in a workshop that does laser cutting, CNC and 3D printing. They search for a file, confirm it is the right one from its preview, and download it, many times a day and usually in the middle of other work. The interface is in Arabic and right to left.

## Product Purpose
A local library that keeps design files organized by project and product and previews them without opening their native programs (STEP, DWG, DXF, RD, G-code and others). Success means finding and confirming the right file in seconds.

## Brand Personality
Calm, clean, precise. The tool should disappear into the task and feel as trustworthy as Linear or Figma.

## Anti-references
- Decorative or themed interfaces, including the earlier drafting-table look with hard shadows and square corners.
- Display fonts in labels and headings.
- Generic admin templates.

## Design Principles
- The file is the hero: chrome stays quiet so thumbnails and drawings carry the screen.
- Scanning beats reading: names, formats and dates are readable at a glance across hundreds of files.
- One accent, used only for primary actions and current state.
- Standard controls and familiar patterns, with every state accounted for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes, visible keyboard focus, full RTL support with correct handling of mixed Arabic and Latin file names, and respect for reduced motion.
